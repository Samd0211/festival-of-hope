ABOUT THE FESTIVAL

What is Ek Pehchan -
Festival of Hope?

Festival of Hope is an inter-school celebration that brings students together to explore creativity, knowledge, collaboration and innovation.

Through a wide range of events and competitions, participants have the opportunity to compete, collaborate and learn from students from different schools.
