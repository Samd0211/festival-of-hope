ROBONGIERS 2026

Engineering

Robongiers is the Design & Technology vertical of Festival of Hope. It gives students a platform to design, build and compete with purpose.

The event consists of two competitions: DesignX · SUB-VERTICAL and RoboTrials · SUB-VERTICAL.

RoboTrials · SUB-VERTICAL is divided into separate Middle School and Senior School categories.

ROBOTRIALS · THREE ROUNDS

Three tests.
One robot.

01

Power Push

Robots face one another in a circular arena approximately 1–1.5 metres in diameter.

The round tests driving control, traction and pushing strength.

A robot wins by pushing its opponent completely outside the arena.

02

Precision Strike

Each team has one minute to burst as many of five designated balloons as possible.

Each balloon counts equally.

Elapsed time is used to break a tie.

03

Velocity Run

Robots race on a marked track that may include curves and small hurdles.

Judges record finishing position and time.

A robot that does not complete the track is recorded as DNF.
