EK PEHCHAN · CHESS LEAGUE 2026

Every move
shapes the game.

The Festival of Hope 2026 Chess League is an inter-school chess championship designed to bring together young minds in a spirit of strategic thinking, healthy competition and sportsmanship.

Through chess, participants have the opportunity to demonstrate critical thinking, concentration, decision-making and resilience while engaging with peers from different schools.

The competition creates a platform where students can learn, compete and grow through meaningful experiences while representing their schools with pride.

Format 5-Round Swiss Team Championship
Junior Division Grades 6–8
Senior Division Grades 9–12
Minimum Team Size 4 players
Rounds 5
Time Control 10 minutes + 10 seconds

TWO DIVISIONS

Two divisions.
One championship spirit.

01

Junior Division

Grades 6–8

Schools may register a Junior team independently or alongside a Senior team.

The Junior Division has its own standings and awards.

02

Senior Division

Grades 9–12

Schools may register a Senior team independently or alongside a Junior team.

The Senior Division has its own standings and awards.

TEAM COMPOSITION

Build your
team.

01

Minimum Team Size

Each team must consist of a minimum of 4 players.

02

Separate Teams

Separate teams must be registered for the Junior and Senior divisions.

03

All registered players will participate throughout the tournament.

04

School Registration

Schools may register teams in either or both categories.

TOURNAMENT FORMAT

Five rounds.
No eliminations.

01

Swiss Team Championship

The competition will be conducted as a 5-round Swiss Team Championship.

02

Every Team Plays

Teams compete in all five rounds with no eliminations.

03

Competitive Pairings

Teams face opponents of similar standing as the competition progresses.

04

Professional Software

Pairings are generated using professional tournament software.

05

Separate Standings

Junior and Senior divisions have separate standings throughout the tournament.

TIME CONTROL

Think fast.
Play smart.

Each game will use a rapid time control of 10 minutes + 10 seconds increment per move.

This format rewards strategic thinking, time management and adaptability under pressure.

Base Time 10 minutes
Increment 10 seconds per move
Format Rapid Chess
Purpose Strategy, time management & adaptability

SCORING

Every game
contributes.

1 POINT

Win

A player win contributes 1 point to the overall team score.

½ POINT

Draw

A draw contributes ½ point to the overall team score.

0 POINTS

Loss

A loss contributes 0 points to the overall team score.

FINAL RANKINGS

Every point
matters.

01

02

03

04
