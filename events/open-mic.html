CREATIVE EXPRESSION · FESTIVAL OF HOPE 2026

Open Mic

Your stage. Your voice. Your moment.

An open performance platform where students can share their creativity through music, words, movement, theatre and more.

OPEN MIC

Step up.
Speak out.
Perform.

Open Mic is open to students from the host school and invited schools.

Every listed performance category may be presented either solo or as a group.

The event provides a platform for students to share their creativity, personality and perspective with an audience.

Eligibility Grades 6-12
Performance Solo / Group
Performance Time Maximum 2–3 minutes
Vertical Date 15 September 2026

PERFORMANCE GUIDELINES

Respect
the stage.

All performances must be school-appropriate and respectful.

Performances should respect cultural, religious, gender and social diversity.

Content that may be considered offensive or inappropriate by a school audience will not be permitted.
