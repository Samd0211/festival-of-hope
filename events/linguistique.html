Linguistique

A celebration of linguistic diversity where language, culture and artistic expression come together through multilingual slam poetry.

LINGUISTIQUE 2026

Many languages.
One voice.

Linguistique seeks to bring diverse cultures and languages together in an increasingly multicultural world.

The event celebrates linguistic competence in Hindi, English and a foreign language, Spanish or French, creating a platform for students to connect languages, cultures and communities through friendly competition.

Through slam poetry, participants explore the power of multilingualism to encourage cross-cultural understanding and creative expression.
