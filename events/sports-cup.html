FOH INTER SCHOOL SWIMMING COMPETITION 2026

One championship.
Countless champions.

The Festival of Hope 2026 Swimming Competition is an inter-school swimming event conducted according to FINA rules.

The competition is divided into two age categories for both boys and girls: U-14 and U-16.

All events will be conducted on a time-trial basis.

Age is determined in completed years as of 31 December 2026.

Date 15 September 2026
Time 9:00 AM onwards
Venue Pathways School Gurgaon
Competition Rules FINA Rules
Competition Format Time Trial
Age Categories U-14 / U-16

ELIGIBILITY & ENTRY

Know your
category.

01

Age Verification

Age is determined in completed years as on 31 December 2026.

02

One Age Group Only

An individual can participate in only one age group.

03

Maximum Individual Events

Each swimmer can participate in a maximum of 3 individual events, including the Individual Medley. Relay participation is excluded from this limit.

04

Two Participants Per Event

Only two participants per school can participate in each event.

05

Minimum Entries

A minimum of 3 entries is required for an event to take place. Otherwise, the event will be cancelled.

DOCUMENTATION

Get the
paperwork right.

01

Date of Birth Certificate

A Date of Birth Certificate / School Bonafide must be attached to the entry form.

02

Institutional Signature

The certificate must be duly signed by the Head of the Educational Institution.

03

Verify Before Registration

Schools must verify the date of birth of all participants against official school records before filling the registration form.

04

Incorrect Age Category

Any participant found competing in a category below their actual age group will be disqualified.
