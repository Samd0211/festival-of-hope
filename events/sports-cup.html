EK PEHCHAN

Three sports.
One platform.

The Ek Pehchan Sports Cup is an inter-school sports competition featuring three distinct sporting disciplines.

Football is a U-19 Boys competition.

Pickleball and Paddle Tennis feature U-19 Boys and U-19 Girls categories, with singles, doubles and mixed doubles competition.

Each sport has its own competition format, rules and designated playing area.

FOOTBALL · U-19 BOYS

Seven on
the pitch.

01

Format

Knockout competition.

02

Team Composition

Seven players, including one goalkeeper, are on the field at any time. Maximum squad size: 15 players.

03

Match Duration

Two 14-minute halves with a 2-minute interval.

04

Substitutions

Unlimited substitutions are permitted according to the instructions of the match officials.

05

Player List

The official player list must be finalised before the team's first match and cannot be changed afterward.

06

Equipment & Identification

Players must wear the required team uniform and safety equipment and carry a valid School ID Card.

PICKLEBALL & PADDLE TENNIS · RULES

Play clean.
Play by the rules.

01

Categories

Boys' Singles, Girls' Singles, Boys' Doubles, Girls' Doubles and Mixed Doubles.

02

Pickleball

Best of three games. Each game is played to 11 points with a winning margin of at least 2 points.

03

Paddle Tennis

Best of three sets. A set is won at 6 games with a margin of at least 2 games. A 7-point tiebreak is used at 6–6.

04

Serving

Both sports use the prescribed underarm serving rules. Paddle Tennis permits one serve attempt per point.

05

Equipment

Sport-specific equipment must comply with the official rules. Non-marking footwear is compulsory for both sports.

GENERAL REGULATIONS

Play fair.
Play safe.

All participants must be currently enrolled students of the participating school.

Every player must carry valid school identification.

A student may represent only one school, and guest players or alumni are not permitted.

A team manager or coach must be present and reachable for every match.

Sportsmanship Expected at all times
Misconduct May result in suspension
Serious Misconduct Possible disqualification
Official Decisions Final

MATCH DAY

Be ready
before the whistle.

01

Arrival

Teams must report to the venue at least 15 minutes before their scheduled match.

02

Grace Period

A 10-minute grace period is allowed for a team unable to field the required number of players.

03

Walkover

Failure to be ready within the grace period results in a walkover.

04

Weather

The Organising Committee may postpone, reschedule, relocate or shorten matches because of unsafe weather or ground conditions.

EK PEHCHAN SPORTS CUP 2026

Ready to
compete?


Step onto the field, represent your school and compete with determination, teamwork and sportsmanship. The Ek Pehchan Sports Cup brings students together through the spirit of competition and the love of sport.

COMPETITION

Schools can register for the sport and category that applies to their students. Prepare your team, know your game and compete for the win.

WHAT IT TAKES

- Teamwork Communicate, support your teammates and work together towards a common goal.
- Sportsmanship Compete with respect, discipline and integrity — on and off the field.

THE CHALLENGE

Train hard, play smart and give your best throughout the competition. Every game brings a new opportunity to compete, improve and make your school proud.
