EK PEHCHAN

Three
Sub-verticals.
One platform.

The Ek Pehchan Sports Cup is an inter-school sports competition featuring three distinct sporting Sub-verticals.

Pickleball and Paddle Tennis feature U-19 Boys and U-19 Girls categories, with singles, doubles and mixed doubles competition.

Swimming features U-14 Boys & Girls and U-16 Boys & Girls categories, with all events conducted on a time-trial basis.

Each Sub-vertical has its own competition format, rules and designated playing area.

SUB-VERTICAL 03 · SWIMMING · ENTRY RULES

Know your
limits.

01

Minimum Entries

A minimum of 3 entries are necessary for participating in an event. The event will otherwise be cancelled.

02

Individual Event Limit

Each swimmer can participate in a maximum of 3 individual events, including IM, excluding Relay.

03

School Entry Limit

Only two participants per school can participate in each event.

04

One Age Group

An individual can participate in only one age group.

05

Incorrect Age Category

Any participant found competing in a category below his/her age group would be disqualified.

GENERAL REGULATIONS

Play fair.
Play safe.

All participants must be currently enrolled students of the participating school.

Every player must carry valid school identification.

A student may represent only one school, and guest players or alumni are not permitted.

A team manager or coach must be present and reachable for every match.

Sportsmanship Expected at all times
Misconduct May result in suspension
Serious Misconduct Possible disqualification
Official Decisions Final

MATCH DAY

Be ready
before play.

01

Arrival

Teams must report to the venue at least 15 minutes before their scheduled match.

02

Grace Period

A 10-minute grace period is allowed for a team unable to field the required number of players.

03

Walkover

Failure to be ready within the grace period results in a walkover.

04

Weather

The Organising Committee may postpone, reschedule, relocate or shorten matches because of unsafe weather or ground conditions.
