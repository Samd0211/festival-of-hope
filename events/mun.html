MODEL PEACE SUMMIT

Where Young Minds
Lead the World

The Model Peace Summit gives students the opportunity to step into the shoes of world leaders and engage with some of the most pressing challenges facing our world. Through discussion, negotiation, collaboration and problem-solving, participants will analyse complex global issues, understand diverse perspectives and work towards practical and sustainable solutions.

The Summit provides a space where young minds can question, deliberate and collaborate, recognising that global challenges rarely have simple answers. By engaging with different viewpoints and navigating competing interests, participants will learn to approach international issues with empathy, responsibility and an openness to dialogue.

Beyond the simulation of global leadership, the Model Summit aims to empower students with the skills to become thoughtful and informed changemakers. Participants will develop diplomacy, critical thinking, communication, collaboration and decision-making skills, while experiencing the challenges and possibilities of working collectively towards a shared vision.

RULES & REGULATIONS

Common
for all delegates.

01

Attendance and Punctuality

Attendance and punctuality are mandatory for all scheduled sessions.

02

Respect and Conduct

Delegates must remain respectful at all times. Attack positions and ideas, never individuals.

No personal insults, mockery, or discriminatory language. The chair may issue a warning or remove a delegate for violations.

03

Speaking and Debate

Delegates may speak only when recognised by the chair. Interrupting another speaker is not permitted.

04

Research and Devices

Laptop to be used only at discretion of chair. Carry your research in hard copy only.

Confidentiality of any sensitive discussion topics should be respected if the school designates them as such.

05

Use of Artificial Intelligence

AI must not be used to generate a position paper, speech, or resolution clause submitted as the student's own work.

AI-generated fake quotes, images, or audio of real leaders or delegates are not permitted. AI may not be used live during debate or caucus to generate arguments or answers on a student's behalf.

All AI-generated facts must be verified against reliable sources.

Where AI has been used to support research, the use must be cited along with prompt used.

DRESS CODE

Dress appropriately
for the committee.

01

Western Formal Attire

Formal western attire is permitted.

Clothing should be neat and appropriate for a formal academic conference.

Formal trousers, shirts, blouses, formal dresses, skirts with stockings, blazers, suits are appropriate.

Closed formal shoes or other formal footwear.
