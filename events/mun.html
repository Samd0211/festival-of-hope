MODEL PEACE SUMMIT

Lead the debate.
Shape the future.

The Model Peace Summit is designed for students in Grades 9–12 and explores one of the most pressing questions surrounding modern conflict and international security.

Delegates will examine the opportunities, risks, ethical concerns and humanitarian implications of emerging technologies in warfare while working towards practical and internationally acceptable solutions.

The Summit aims to foster critical thinking, informed debate, diplomatic skills and a deeper understanding of international cooperation and peacebuilding.

Eligibility Grades 9–12
Core Issue AI & Autonomous Technologies in Armed Conflict
Date 15 September 2026
Venue Third Floor
Opening Speech 90 seconds
Position Paper Required

CORE ISSUE

Regulating AI
in warfare.

01

Central Question

How should the international community regulate AI and autonomous technologies being used in armed conflict?

02

What Delegates Examine

Delegates will examine the opportunities, risks, ethical concerns and humanitarian implications of emerging technologies in warfare.

03

The Goal

Delegates will work towards practical and internationally acceptable approaches to the regulation of these technologies.

Each delegate will deliver a 90-second opening speech presenting their assigned country's position, concerns and priorities on the core issue.

Delegates should clearly communicate their country's perspective while identifying the key concerns and priorities that will guide their participation in the Summit.

RULES & REGULATIONS

Common
for all delegates.

01

Attendance and Punctuality

Attendance and punctuality are mandatory for all scheduled sessions.

02

Respect and Conduct

Delegates must remain respectful at all times. Attack positions and ideas, never individuals.

No personal insults, mockery, or discriminatory language. The chair may issue a warning or remove a delegate for violations.

03

Speaking and Debate

Delegates may speak only when recognised by the chair. Interrupting another speaker is not permitted.

04

Research and Devices

Laptops may be used only at the discretion of the chair. Carry your research in hard copy where required.

Confidentiality of any sensitive discussion topics should be respected if the school designates them as such.

05

Use of Artificial Intelligence

AI must not be used to generate a position paper, speech, or resolution clause submitted as the student's own work.

AI-generated fake quotes, images, or audio of real leaders or delegates are not permitted. AI may not be used live during debate or caucus to generate arguments or answers on a student's behalf.

All AI-generated facts must be verified against reliable sources.

Where AI has been used to support research, the use must be cited along with the prompt used.
